## 1 · Role Definition & Scope

### What the CHRO actually does in China

The CHRO in a Chinese enterprise is simultaneously a **strategic talent architect**, a **compliance navigator**, and increasingly an **organizational efficiency engineer** (组织效能). Unlike Western counterparts who may focus primarily on culture and talent development, the China CHRO must balance:

1. **Regulatory tightrope** — Labor law enforcement has shifted dramatically post-996 crackdown. Social insurance (社保) audits are now algorithmic. The CHRO owns the risk of non-compliance penalties that can reach 3x back-payment.

2. **Party-state interface** (国企 and 混合所有制) — In state-owned or state-adjacent enterprises, 党管干部 (the Party manages cadres) means senior appointments require Party committee approval. The CHRO must navigate dual governance.

3. **Organizational vitality** (组织活力) — A uniquely Chinese framing: preventing "big company disease" (大企业病). ByteDance's 梁汝波 made "去肥增瘦" (remove fat, add muscle) a company-wide mandate in 2023-2024, and the CHRO operationalizes this.

4. **AI talent war** — Top AI researchers command ¥200-500万 base + equity packages. This creates massive internal equity distortion that the CHRO must manage without demoralizing the 95% who earn 1/10th of that.

5. **降本增效** (reduce cost, increase efficiency) — The post-2022 macro environment made this the dominant CEO mandate. The CHRO must cut headcount while maintaining engagement and avoiding labor disputes that trend on 脉脉 or 微博.

### Title variations and what they signal

| Title | Signal |
|-------|--------|
| HRD (人力资源总监) | Mid-cap company; operational focus; likely reports to VP or COO |
| HRVP (人力资源副总裁) | Large company; strategic seat; reports to CEO |
| CHO (首席人才官) | Tech company; talent-acquisition heavy mandate |
| CPO (Chief People Officer) | Foreign-influenced; culture + experience focus |
| 组织发展VP | OD-centric; transformation mandate |

### Span of influence

- **Headcount decisions**: Final approval on all hiring above P7/M2 level in most tech firms
- **Compensation architecture**: Owns the total rewards philosophy and band structure
- **Culture codification**: Defines values, conducts culture audits, owns employer brand
- **Organizational design**: Restructuring, BU creation/merger, reporting line changes
- **Labor relations**: Union interface (especially in manufacturing, aviation like Cathay Pacific)
- **HR digital transformation**: HRIS selection, people analytics, AI-in-HR adoption

## 2 · Strategic Priorities (FY2024-2026)

### Priority 1: 降本增效 — Efficiency without implosion

**Context**: Post-2022 economic slowdown forced nearly every major Chinese tech and manufacturing firm into headcount optimization. The CHRO must deliver 15-30% cost reduction while:
- Avoiding mass layoff PR crises (社会性事件)
- Retaining top 10% performers
- Maintaining 脉脉/Glassdoor employer brand scores

**Tactical manifestation**:
- 末位淘汰 (forced ranking elimination) — 理想汽车 notoriously applies harsh 10% bottom culling
- "毕业" euphemism for layoffs (ByteDance style)
- Shifting from N+1 to negotiated separation packages
- Converting FTEs to outsourced/派遣 workers for non-core functions

### Priority 2: AI talent acquisition & retention

**Context**: China's AI talent pool is approximately 50,000 senior practitioners competing across BAT, ByteDance, 华为, and hundreds of startups. The CHRO faces:
- Base salary inflation of 30-50% YoY for top AI talent
- Equity expectations benchmarked to US packages
- Internal fairness complaints from non-AI engineers earning 1/3 the comp
- Poaching cycles measured in months, not years

**Tactical manifestation**:
- Dual-track career ladders (技术序列 vs 管理序列) with AI-specific bands
- 股权激励 (equity incentive) plans with 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff
- "特殊人才" approval tracks that bypass normal comp bands
- University lab partnerships (清华, 北大, 中科院) for pipeline

### Priority 3: Compliance modernization

**Context**: Post-996, labor enforcement shifted from complaint-driven to proactive algorithmic monitoring. Key pressure points:
- 社保 full-base payment enforcement (many firms historically paid on minimum base)
- 加班 (overtime) recording and compensation
- 灵活用工 (flexible employment) classification risks
- Data privacy (个人信息保护法 PIPL) for employee data

### Priority 4: Organizational vitality & culture

**Context**: As firms scale past 10,000 employees, "大企业病" sets in. The CHRO must:
- Maintain startup energy (海尔's 人单合一 model — self-organizing micro-enterprises)
- Implement OKR/KPI hybrids that drive accountability without bureaucracy
- Combat the "35岁危机" narrative that demoralizes mid-career talent
- Build 雇主品牌 (employer brand) on 脉脉, Boss直聘, and 小红书

### Priority 5: Digital HR transformation

**Context**: CHROs are under pressure to demonstrate HR's own efficiency through technology:
- AI-powered recruitment screening (reducing time-to-hire from 45 to 20 days)
- People analytics dashboards for attrition prediction
- Digital onboarding reducing HR SSC headcount
- ChatGPT/大模型 applications in L&D, policy Q&A, and performance review drafting

## 3 · Five CHRO Archetypes in China

### Archetype A: 国企组织部长型 (State Enterprise Party-Cadre Manager)

**Profile**: Senior Party member, often rotated from 组织部 or 纪委. Deep understanding of 党管干部 principles. Manages dual-track: market-hire professionals AND Party-appointed cadres.

**Exemplar pattern**: 海康威视 — 50,000+ employees, state parent (中电海康), must balance market competitiveness with state governance requirements.

**Key concerns**:
- 干部选拔 (cadre selection) aligning Party requirements with business needs
- Political study time (政治学习) integration without productivity loss
- Foreign talent management under state-security constraints
- Compensation ceilings that lose talent to private sector

**Buying behavior**: Conservative, committee-driven, long procurement cycles (6-12 months). Requires local data residency. Prefers domestic vendors (信创 compliance).

### Archetype B: 互联网效能型 (Internet Efficiency Optimizer)

**Profile**: Ex-consulting (McKinsey/BCG) or scaled through a hyper-growth tech firm. Data-driven. Speaks in headcount efficiency ratios and revenue-per-employee.

**Exemplar pattern**: ByteDance under 梁汝波 — OKR discipline, "去肥增瘦" organizational trimming, bi-monthly org reviews, aggressive performance differentiation.

**Key concerns**:
- Human efficiency ratio (人效比) benchmarking vs peers
- Speed of organizational restructuring
- Talent density maintenance during downsizing
- Avoiding 脉脉 PR fires from disgruntled employees

**Buying behavior**: Fast decision-making if ROI is clear. Wants pilot-to-scale in 90 days. Will pay premium for proven solutions. Allergic to "heavy implementation."

### Archetype C: 制造业合规型 (Manufacturing Compliance Guardian)

**Profile**: Labor law specialist background. Manages 10,000+ blue-collar workers across multiple sites. Primary worry is labor disputes, work injuries, and regulatory penalties.

**Exemplar pattern**: Shein — massive supply chain workforce, international labor compliance scrutiny, ESG reporting pressure from Western buyers.

**Key concerns**:
- Labor dispute prevention (集体劳动争议)
- Work injury rates and insurance costs
- 派遣工/外包 ratio compliance (legally capped at 10% for 派遣)
- International audit readiness (SA8000, BSCI)
- Seasonal workforce scaling (旺季用工)

**Buying behavior**: Cost-sensitive. Needs proven ROI in dispute reduction or compliance cost avoidance. Prefers modular solutions that can deploy site-by-site. Long vendor evaluation.

### Archetype D: 新势力激进型 (New Forces Aggressive Performer)

**Profile**: Young (35-42), aggressive, views HR as a performance weapon. Willing to push legal boundaries on performance management. Often in NEV, robotics, or AI startups.

**Exemplar pattern**: 理想汽车 — harsh performance culture, public 361 ranking, "不养闲人" philosophy, rapid scaling then rapid optimization.

**Key concerns**:
- Performance differentiation that's legally defensible
- Scaling from 5,000 to 30,000 then back to 20,000 within 2 years
- Building culture that attracts "自驱型" (self-driven) talent
- Stock option plans that actually retain (not just attract)
- IPO-readiness HR infrastructure

**Buying behavior**: Impatient. Wants results in 30-60 days. Will champion internally if product delivers. High churn risk if implementation stalls. Budget available but attention span short.

### Archetype E: 国际化桥梁型 (Globalization Bridge Builder)

**Profile**: Overseas education or MNC background. Managing cross-border workforce. Balancing China HQ expectations with local labor laws in SE Asia, Middle East, or Europe.

**Exemplar pattern**: Cathay Pacific (HK-mainland crew management, union dynamics); TikTok/ByteDance (global HR with China HQ); Shein (global compliance).

**Key concerns**:
- Multi-jurisdiction labor compliance
- Cross-cultural management frameworks
- Global mobility and immigration
- Headquarters vs local autonomy tension
- Union/works council navigation (unfamiliar to China-trained HR)

**Buying behavior**: Sophisticated buyer. Benchmarks against global vendors. Needs multi-language, multi-entity capability. Willing to pay for integration quality. Decision involves global and regional stakeholders.

## 5 · Pain Points (Deep Dive)

### Pain Point 1: Compensation distortion from AI talent war

**The problem**: A senior AI researcher (3-5 years experience) commands ¥200-500万 total comp. A senior backend engineer with 8 years commands ¥80-150万. This 3-5x gap within the same engineering organization creates:
- Visible resentment on internal forums
- "Why not pivot to AI" career pressure that hollows out core engineering
- Budget allocation conflicts with business units
- Equity dilution concerns from finance

**Emotional weight**: The CHRO feels caught between CEO demands ("get me AI talent at any cost") and organizational fairness principles they've spent careers building.

**Current coping**: Separate AI subsidiaries/labs with different comp structures; "special talent" bands hidden from normal HR systems; deferred equity with long vesting to reduce cash impact.

### Pain Point 2: 35岁危机 and mid-career talent exodus

**The problem**: China's tech industry has created a cultural narrative that workers become "too expensive and too slow" after 35. This creates:
- Self-fulfilling prophecy as mid-career talent disengages
- Loss of institutional knowledge
- Age discrimination lawsuits (increasing post-2023)
- Employer brand damage on 脉脉

**Emotional weight**: CHROs privately acknowledge the "35岁" problem is real in their orgs but cannot publicly validate it without legal/PR exposure.

**Current coping**: "专家序列" (expert tracks) for experienced ICs; internal entrepreneurship programs (海尔 人单合一); managed transitions to less intense roles; early retirement packages disguised as "career transition support."

### Pain Point 3: 脉脉/social media reputation management

**The problem**: 脉脉 (China's LinkedIn + Glassdoor + Blind combined) gives employees anonymous platforms to air grievances. A single viral post about layoffs, toxic culture, or unfair treatment can:
- Tank recruitment acceptance rates within days
- Trigger labor bureau investigations
- Force CEO public responses
- Create competitive disadvantage in talent market

**Emotional weight**: CHROs describe "脉脉 anxiety" — constantly monitoring posts, never knowing when an internal decision becomes a public crisis.

**Current coping**: Employer branding teams dedicated to 脉脉; "alumni" relationship management to prevent bitter posts; rapid response protocols; some firms attempt (controversial) legal action against anonymous posters.

### Pain Point 4: Post-996 compliance in still-intense cultures

**The problem**: The legal framework now prohibits 996 (9am-9pm, 6 days). But many firms, especially in NEV and AI, still operate at extreme intensity. The CHRO must:
- Maintain legal compliance on paper
- Not destroy the competitive intensity that drives results
- Manage the gap between "official hours" and "actual hours"
- Handle employees who weaponize labor law against performance management

**Emotional weight**: "I know we're not truly compliant, but if I enforce strictly, we lose to competitors who don't." This is the CHRO's private ethical tension.

**Current coping**: "弹性工作制" (flexible work system) approvals from labor bureau; "综合工时制" for eligible roles; shifting to output-based (not hours-based) performance metrics; quiet acceptance of "voluntary" overtime with compensatory time off.

### Pain Point 5: HR digital transformation that actually works

**The problem**: CHROs have invested in multiple HR tech systems (北森, Moka, SAP SuccessFactors, 飞书People) but face:
- Integration failures between systems
- Low manager adoption of self-service tools
- Data quality issues making analytics unreliable
- AI/大模型 hype without clear use cases
- Vendor lock-in with poor customization

**Emotional weight**: "I've spent ¥2000万 on HR tech over 3 years and my CEO still asks me for data I can't produce in real-time."

**Current coping**: Rip-and-replace fatigue; settling for "good enough" rather than best-of-breed; building internal tools teams; piloting AI narrowly (resume screening, chatbot Q&A) before broader rollout.

### Pain Point 6: 股权激励 design in volatile markets

**The problem**: Equity incentives (股权激励) are the primary retention tool for senior talent, but:
- IPO timelines have extended from 3 to 5-7 years
- Secondary market liquidity dried up post-2022
- Employees discount illiquid equity heavily (treating it as "画饼")
- Tax treatment on exercise is punitive (up to 45% marginal)
- Clawback clauses create legal disputes on departure

**Emotional weight**: "Our equity is supposed to be our golden handcuff, but employees treat it as worthless paper because they've seen too many friends' options go underwater."

**Current coping**: Cash retention bonuses replacing some equity; shorter vesting schedules (3-year instead of 4); phantom equity for non-IPO entities; creative structures combining ESOP + 合伙人 fund models.

## 6 · Objections to New Solutions (with Rebuttals)

### Objection 1: "我们刚做完系统切换，团队没有精力再折腾"
*("We just finished a system migration, the team has no energy for another change")*

**What they really mean**: Change fatigue is real; the last implementation was painful and under-delivered. They fear repeating that experience.

**Effective rebuttal angle**: Acknowledge the pain. Position as additive/plugin rather than rip-and-replace. Show implementation that requires <20% of previous project effort. Offer phased approach starting with one module that delivers quick wins (e.g., 30-day pilot on one pain point).

### Objection 2: "数据安全怎么保证？员工数据是最敏感的"
*("How do you guarantee data security? Employee data is the most sensitive")*

**What they really mean**: Post-PIPL, CHROs are personally liable for data breaches. They've seen peers fired over data incidents. This is existential risk, not checklist compliance.

**Effective rebuttal angle**: Lead with architecture (data residency, encryption, access controls). Provide PIPL-specific compliance documentation. Offer on-premise or private cloud deployment options. Reference similar-scale deployments with named (with permission) references. Third-party security audit reports (等保2.0 certification).

### Objection 3: "AI招聘怕有偏见，出了事我担责"
*("AI recruitment might have bias, and I'm the one held responsible if something goes wrong")*

**What they really mean**: The CHRO has no way to audit AI decisions. If a discrimination case emerges, they cannot defend "the algorithm did it." Regulatory guidance on AI-in-HR is still forming.

**Effective rebuttal angle**: Explainability features that produce audit trails. Human-in-the-loop design where AI recommends but humans decide. Bias testing reports with statistical evidence. Position as "augmentation not automation" — the CHRO retains control and can demonstrate due diligence.

### Objection 4: "这个方案太贵了，老板让我降本"
*("This solution is too expensive, my boss wants me to cut costs")*

**What they really mean**: They may actually want the product but need ammunition to justify spend in a cost-cutting environment. Help them build the business case.

**Effective rebuttal angle**: Frame as cost reduction tool, not cost addition. Calculate: "If this reduces attrition by 2%, that saves ¥X in replacement costs." Offer gain-sharing or outcome-based pricing. Show payback period <12 months. Compare to cost of the problem (labor disputes at ¥10-20万 each × volume).

### Objection 5: "我们是国企，流程你懂的"
*("We're a state enterprise, you understand the process")*

**What they really mean**: Decision requires multiple committee approvals (党委会, 总经理办公会, 信息化委员会). Timeline is 6-12 months minimum. Vendor must meet 信创 requirements. Personal risk of championing = high; reward of championing = low.

**Effective rebuttal angle**: Demonstrate patience and process respect. Provide materials formatted for committee presentations. Offer free pilot/POC that generates internal evidence before formal procurement. Ensure 信创 compatibility. Find internal champion at 分管领导 level who can shepherd through committees.

### Objection 6: "现在不是好时机，等业务稳定了再说"
*("Now isn't a good time, let's wait until the business stabilizes")*

**What they really mean**: Either genuinely overwhelmed, or politely declining. Need to distinguish between the two.

**Effective rebuttal angle**: If genuine timing issue: propose a "design now, implement Q+1" approach that uses current bandwidth minimally. If polite decline: ask "What would need to be true for this to become a priority?" — this surfaces the real objection. For urgency: "Every month of delay = X more regrettable departures / Y more compliance exposure."

## 7 · Buying Dynamics & Procurement

### Budget structure

| Item | Typical allocation |
|------|-------------------|
| Total HR tech budget | ¥500万-3000万/year (10K+ employee firm) |
| HRIS/HCM platform | 40-50% of HR tech budget |
| Recruitment tools | 20-25% |
| L&D platforms | 15-20% |
| Analytics/new tools | 10-15% |
| Consulting/advisory | Variable, often separate budget |

### Decision-making unit

| Role | Influence | Concern |
|------|-----------|---------|
| CHRO/HRVP | Final HR decision | Strategic fit, team capacity, career risk |
| CTO/CIO | Veto power on tech | Integration, security, architecture |
| CFO | Budget approval | ROI, TCO, payment terms |
| HRIS Manager | Heavy influence on shortlist | Usability, implementation effort, vendor support |
| CEO | Sponsors strategic HR initiatives | Business impact only |
| 党委/Board (国企) | Approval authority | Compliance, data sovereignty, 信创 |

### Procurement timeline

| Company type | Typical cycle | Key gates |
|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Private tech (互联网) | 2-4 months | CHRO + CTO alignment → CFO sign-off |
| Listed private (上市民企) | 3-6 months | Board/audit committee for large purchases |
| State-owned (国企/央企) | 6-18 months | 信息化委员会 → 党委会 → 招标流程 |
| MNC China entity | 3-8 months | Global procurement + local validation |
| Startup/scaleup | 2-6 weeks | CEO + CHRO co-decision |

### What accelerates deals

- **Peer reference from same industry**: "XX公司 already uses this" is the strongest accelerator
- **Quick pilot**: Low-commitment 30-day proof with measurable outcome
- **Integration proof**: Live demo connecting to their existing 飞书/钉钉/企业微信 + HRIS
- **Compliance documentation pre-built**: PIPL assessment, 等保 certification, data residency proof
- **Executive sponsorship content**: CEO-level case studies that help CHRO "sell up"

### What kills deals

- Requiring IT resources the CHRO doesn't control
- Implementation timelines >6 months before first value
- Vendor instability (startup with <2 years funding runway)
- Poor mobile experience (managers live on 飞书/钉钉/微信)
- Inability to produce Chinese-language contracts and support

## 8 · Discovery Questions for Engaging CHROs

### Opening (understand mandate)

1. "您今年组织工作的第一优先级是什么——是效率提升、人才获取、还是合规风险管控？"
   *(What's your #1 org priority this year — efficiency, talent acquisition, or compliance risk?)*

2. "贵公司目前的人效比在行业里处于什么水平？CEO对这个指标的期望是？"
   *(Where does your revenue-per-employee sit vs industry? What's the CEO's expectation?)*

3. "过去一年，组织架构做了几次大的调整？"
   *(How many major org restructures in the past year?)*

### Pain exploration

4. "在关键人才保留上，目前最让您头疼的是哪个群体？AI人才、中层管理者、还是业务骨干？"
   *(Which talent segment gives you the biggest retention headache — AI talent, mid-management, or business backbone?)*

5. "社保合规这块，有没有历史欠缴需要处理的？现在是全额基数缴纳吗？"
   *(On social insurance compliance, any historical underpayment issues? Are you on full-base payment now?)*

6. "如果脉脉上明天出一条关于贵公司的负面帖子，你们的响应机制是什么？"
   *(If a negative post about your company trends on 脉脉 tomorrow, what's your response mechanism?)*

### Solution fit

7. "您现在用的HR系统是什么？最大的痛点在哪——数据打通、流程效率、还是分析能力？"
   *(What HR system are you on? Biggest pain — data integration, process efficiency, or analytics?)*

8. "如果有一个方案能帮您把关键岗位的招聘周期从45天降到25天，值多少预算？"
   *(If a solution cut critical-role hiring cycle from 45 to 25 days, what budget would that justify?)*

### Buying process

9. "这类项目在贵公司通常需要哪些部门会签？IT和财务的参与节点是？"
   *(Which departments need to co-sign on this type of project? When do IT and Finance get involved?)*

10. "如果我们做一个30天的试点，您觉得哪个场景最能快速看到效果？"
    *(If we did a 30-day pilot, which use case would show results fastest?)*

## 9 · Communication Style & Engagement Preferences

### How CHROs prefer to receive information

| Dimension | Preference |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Format** | Executive summary (1-page) + detailed appendix; visual frameworks preferred over dense text |
| **Language** | Chinese-primary; bilingual acceptable for MNC context; avoid untranslated English jargon |
| **Data** | Benchmarks against named peers in same industry/scale; absolute numbers > percentages alone |
| **Channel** | WeChat (relationship), Email (formal), 飞书/钉钉 (if internal platform) |
| **Meeting style** | 30-min initial; value density over relationship-building in first meeting (differs from CFO) |
| **Follow-up** | WeChat message with meeting summary + clear next step within 2 hours |

### Language patterns that resonate

**Use these phrases**:
- "组织效能提升" (organizational effectiveness improvement)
- "人才密度" (talent density) — borrowed from Netflix/ByteDance vocabulary
- "数据驱动的人才决策" (data-driven talent decisions)
- "合规风险前置管理" (proactive compliance risk management)
- "业务伙伴" (business partner) — HRBPs aspire to this identity
- "降本增效的同时保护雇主品牌" (cut costs while protecting employer brand)

**Avoid these phrases**:
- "人力资源管理" in isolation — sounds administrative, not strategic
- "裁员" directly — use "组织优化" or "人员结构调整"
- "加班" in positive framing — post-996 sensitivity
- "性价比高的方案" — implies cheap, which implies low-quality for a function fighting for strategic credibility
- Untranslated English acronyms without explanation

### Emotional triggers (positive)

- Being treated as a **strategic business leader**, not an administrative function
- Solutions that make them **look innovative** to the CEO
- Peer recognition (conference speaking, case study publication)
- Evidence that their function **drives measurable business outcomes**

### Emotional triggers (negative)

- Being treated as a cost center or administrative support
- Vendors who only talk to IT/Finance and treat HR as "user"
- Solutions that add workload to their already-stretched team
- Being surprised by compliance risks they should have known about
- Public embarrassment on 脉脉 or in media

## 10 · Reference Company Patterns

### 海尔 — 人单合一 (RenDanHeYi)

**HR model**: Radical decentralization. 4,000+ "micro-enterprises" (小微) within the group. Each has P&L ownership. HR is embedded, not centralized. Employees are "创客" (makers), not workers.

**CHRO lesson**: In this model, the CHRO designs the ecosystem rules but doesn't control individual talent decisions. The challenge is maintaining fairness and compliance across 4,000 autonomous units.

**Relevance for vendors**: Solutions must be configurable at micro-unit level while providing group-level visibility. One-size-fits-all fails here.

### ByteDance — OKR + 去肥增瘦

**HR model**: Highly systematized OKR with bi-monthly reviews. 360-degree feedback mandatory. Performance calibration sessions are intense. Post-梁汝波, "organizational efficiency" became the dominant HR narrative.

**CHRO lesson**: HR as a performance engineering function. Every process measured for cycle time and effectiveness. HR team itself subject to efficiency scrutiny.

**Relevance for vendors**: Must demonstrate measurable efficiency gains. "Soft" value propositions (culture, engagement) must be connected to hard metrics (attrition cost, time-to-fill, revenue-per-head).

### 理想汽车 — Harsh performance culture

**HR model**: Aggressive 361 distribution (30% exceeds, 60% meets, 10% below). Below-threshold triggers PIP or exit within one cycle. Public internal rankings. "不养闲人" (no room for freeloaders) as stated philosophy.

**CHRO lesson**: Legal risk is high. Each performance-based termination is a potential labor arbitration case. The CHRO must build documentation processes that make PIPs legally defensible.

**Relevance for vendors**: Performance management tools with audit trails, legal-compliant documentation templates, and labor arbitration risk scoring are high-value.

### Shein — Supply chain labor compliance

**HR model**: Complex multi-tier supply chain with labor compliance pressure from international buyers. Must demonstrate SA8000/BSCI compliance while operating in cost-competitive Guangzhou garment ecosystem.

**CHRO lesson**: International scrutiny creates different compliance standard than domestic-only firms. ESG reporting on labor practices is now a business-critical function (affects buyer relationships).

**Relevance for vendors**: Supply chain labor monitoring, audit management systems, multi-language compliance reporting, and worker voice platforms are relevant.

### 海康威视 — State-parent tech giant

**HR model**: 50,000+ employees. Parent is 中电海康 (state-owned). Must comply with 党管干部 for senior roles while competing with pure-private firms for engineering talent. Dual compensation tracks: market-rate for tech talent, state-guided for administrative/Party roles.

**CHRO lesson**: The CHRO navigates two worlds simultaneously. Procurement follows state rules (信创 preference, 招标 process). But talent strategy must be market-competitive.

**Relevance for vendors**: Must meet 信创 requirements (domestic tech stack). Data residency non-negotiable. Long sales cycle but large deal sizes. Relationship-building with 信息化部门 is essential.

### Cathay Pacific — Union dynamics in Greater China

**HR model**: Unionized cabin crew and pilots with collective bargaining agreements. Post-COVID restructuring created massive labor relations challenges. Cross-border workforce (HK-mainland-international).

**CHRO lesson**: Union management is rare in mainland China tech but critical in aviation, manufacturing, and Hong Kong entities. The CHRO must balance collective agreements with business flexibility.

**Relevance for vendors**: Workforce scheduling, union agreement compliance tracking, multi-jurisdiction payroll, and crew resource management are relevant domains.

## 12 · Summary: What Wins with a China CHRO

### The winning positioning formula

```
"We help you [specific efficiency/compliance/talent metric] 
 without [their biggest fear: implementation burden / compliance risk / employer brand damage]
 proven at [named peer reference in same industry]
 deployable in [timeline that respects their reality]"
```

### Top 5 rules of engagement

1. **Lead with 人效** — Every conversation should connect to revenue-per-employee or cost-per-hire. This is the language CEOs use with CHROs, and therefore the language CHROs value.

2. **Respect the compliance layer** — Never position a solution that could increase regulatory exposure. Always show how you reduce or maintain compliance posture.

3. **Show, don't tell** — 30-day pilots with measurable outcomes beat 60-page proposals. CHROs are tired of vendor promises.

4. **Know their tech stack** — Ask about 飞书/钉钉/企业微信, existing HRIS, and integration requirements in the first meeting. A solution that doesn't connect to their ecosystem is dead on arrival.

5. **Protect their reputation** — Everything you help them implement will be visible to employees and potentially to 脉脉. If a solution creates employee friction, it reflects on the CHRO personally.
