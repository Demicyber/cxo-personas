# CXO Persona Library — China Market Index (中国市场高管画像索引)

## Overview

This document indexes **19 China-market C-suite and senior executive personas** organized across 7 categories. The library powers AI sales agents that help sales teams prepare for meetings with Chinese enterprise executives — enabling rapid persona identification, priority alignment, and engagement coaching tailored to each executive's mindset within the China business environment.

**Key differences from the Global persona set:**
- Three China-unique roles with no Western equivalent: 出海VP, 政府关系VP, 事业群总裁
- All personas reflect China-specific regulatory environment (网络安全法, 数据安全法, 个保法)
- Research base: 12 China/HK companies (Anker, 海康威视, 海尔, 理想, Shein, ByteDance, 禾观, Cathay Pacific, WeBull, 港交所, 猎豹, 腾讯游戏)
- Title variants use Chinese terms (many executives don't use English C-suite titles)

## How to Use This Document

### For AI Agents and LLMs

When preparing for a meeting with a China-market executive:

1. **Identify the persona** using the Title Mapping Guide (中文职位映射) below
2. **Load the full persona file** — the summaries here are insufficient for preparation
3. **Cross-reference** the Industry Context Map if the executive's company is in a specific sector

### For Human Users

These personas provide the **audience intelligence layer** for China-market sales preparation. They reflect how Chinese executives actually think, communicate, and make decisions — which differs significantly from global assumptions.

## Cross-Persona Dynamics (跨角色协作动态)

### Common Buying Committees in China

| Deal Type | Typical Committee | Power Dynamic |
|---|---|---|
| **ERP / 信创** | CIO (lead) + CFO (budget) + BU Heads (users) | CIO drives, but BU Heads can veto if UX poor |
| **AI Platform / 大模型** | VP AI (lead) + CTO (arch) + BU Head (use case) + CFO | VP AI proposes, CFO gates, BU Head validates ROI |
| **Cybersecurity / 等保** | CISO (lead) + CIO + CCO + VP Gov Relations | CISO decides tech, Gov Relations ensures政策alignment |
| **出海 SaaS** | VP International (lead) + CTO (dual-stack) + CMO (brand) | VP Intl owns budget, CTO gates architecture |
| **HR Tech** | CHRO (lead) + CIO (integration) + CFO (ROI) | CHRO drives, CFO challenges headcount ROI |
| **Supply Chain Platform** | VP Supply Chain (lead) + COO + CIO + VP Manufacturing | Cross-functional; COO often final approver |
| **Compliance / RegTech** | CCO (lead) + General Counsel + CISO + CIO | CCO owns requirement, CIO owns implementation |

### China-Specific Power Dynamics

- **BU Head often outranks functional C-suite** in purchasing power (独立P&L)
- **VP Gov Relations** is a gatekeeper for any deal involving government customers or subsidies
- **CEO/Founder** involvement threshold is LOWER in Chinese companies — they get pulled into ¥5M+ deals
- **赛马机制** means you may sell to COMPETING BUs within the same company simultaneously
