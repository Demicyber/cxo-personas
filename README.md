# CXO Personas

**Audience intelligence for sales teams preparing for executive meetings.**

A library of 19 deeply-researched CXO persona profiles designed for AI sales agents and human practitioners. Each persona captures how a specific C-suite role thinks, what they prioritize, and how to engage them — with dedicated China market (🇨🇳) content and industry-specific depth.

## What's Inside

### 19 Persona Profiles (`references/`)

| Category | Personas |
|----------|----------|
| **Strategic Leadership** | CEO, COO, Chief Digital Officer (CDxO) |
| **Finance & Operations** | CFO, Head of Procurement |
| **Technology & Digital** | CTO, CIO, CISO, CAIO, Chief Data Officer, Chief Analytics Officer |
| **Revenue & Growth** | CRO, CMO, CPO, VP of Sales |
| **People & Culture** | CHRO |
| **Risk & Legal** | General Counsel (GC) |
| **Innovation & Manufacturing** | Chief Innovation Officer (CINO), VP of Manufacturing |

### Each Persona Includes

1. **Archetypes** — "Postures, Not Industries" behavioral patterns
2. **Three Time Horizons** — What they care about at 90 days, 1 year, 3+ years
3. **Four-Way Pull** — The competing demands that shape their decisions
4. **🇨🇳 China Market Sections** — Guanxi dynamics, local tech ecosystem, regulatory landscape, cultural nuances
5. **Industry Deep-Dives** — 8-industry tables with named companies, vendor ecosystems, pain points, and Sales切入 (entry points)
6. **Engagement Guidance** — How to approach, what language resonates, what to avoid

### Supporting Files

- `references/INDEX.md` — Full directory with title mapping, topic routing, and cross-persona dynamics
- `SKILL.md` — AI agent skill definition for loading personas in context
- `research/` — Design decisions, China research notes, ablation testing

## Usage

### For AI Agents (Hermes, etc.)

Load the `cxo-personas` skill, then:
1. Identify the target executive's role
2. Load the full persona profile
3. Apply insights to meeting prep, engagement plans, or call scripts

### For Humans

Browse `references/INDEX.md` for the full collection, or open any persona file directly. Use the Title Mapping Guide if your target's job title doesn't match a standard C-suite label.
